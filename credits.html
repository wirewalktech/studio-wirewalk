Provenance

Where every picture on this site came from.

A photography studio that is vague about the provenance of the images on its own website has no standing to be precise about anything else. So: {{ site.data.photos | size }} frames are published here. Those that are the studio’s own are marked as such on the page they appear on. Those that are not are labelled “reference frame” in their alt text and in a note on the section, and every one of them is listed below with its source and licence.

03 — Licensed reference frames

Illustrating the services the archive does not cover.

A second set of {{ lic_count }} published frames, derived from the {{ lic_sources }} licensed source photographs listed below — one is used at two aspect ratios, which is why the counts differ by one. Added to illustrate family, newborn and pet sessions, headshots in a workplace, product, video and audio, and to build out the real-estate section — all categories where the studio’s published archive is thin or where commissioned work is confidential. They were sourced through Openverse, which returns licence, creator and source as structured data rather than as a claim on a web page — so the table below is generated from that data when the site builds, not typed out by hand. A credit somebody retypes is a credit that eventually goes wrong.

One category is deliberately not filled. There is no wedding and no conference photography in this set: searching for it under an acceptable licence returned political ceremonies, military decorations and museum objects, and none of that would honestly stand in for wedding or event coverage. The five party frames here are used as what they are — a cake, a sparkler, confetti — and the events and weddings sections are carried by their words instead. That category needs the photographer’s own work and is waiting for it.

Licences are CC0, public-domain mark or CC-BY only. Share-alike work was excluded deliberately, so there is never a question about what a page containing one of these becomes. {% if by_count > 0 %}{{ by_count }} of the {{ lic_sources }} require attribution under CC-BY; the creator, licence version and source page for each are in the table, which is the attribution.{% endif %}

None of this is the studio’s work. Each is labelled “Reference frame” in its alt text, flagged on the section it appears in, and never placed beside copy claiming the studio shot it.

04 — Everything else

Type and build.

Typography

Two faces

Instrument Serif for display and Inter for text, both under the SIL Open Font License, served from Google Fonts with system fallback stacks.

Build

Jekyll on GitHub Pages

Static pages, inline CSS, and about forty lines of vanilla JavaScript for the menu and the scroll reveal. No frameworks, no libraries, no analytics, no cookies, no trackers. The contact form posts directly to FormSubmit and nowhere else.

Images

Derived, not uploaded

Every frame is developed, cropped to its display ratio and exported to WebP with a JPEG fallback at several widths, so a phone downloads a phone-sized file. Nothing on this site hotlinks an image from another host.
