03 — Quoted, not listed

Some work cannot honestly carry a list price.

A wedding is not a unit. Hours of coverage, a second shooter, an album, a rehearsal dinner and a timeline that runs from morning prep to the last dance vary so much between two weddings that a single number on a page would be a guess dressed up as a price. Scripted film is the same: the treatment decides the budget, not the other way round. So these are quoted after we talk, in writing, before anything is booked.

04 — For companies

If your finance team needs an invoice or a purchase order.

Commercial work — team headshots, a workplace day, product, property, video and audio — can be placed on a purchase order and invoiced instead of paid by card. Ask here and an invoice, W-9 or supplier form comes back the same working day; the dates are held while it goes through your system.

Family, newborn, portrait, pet and wedding bookings are card only. Nobody raises a purchase order for a family session, and pretending otherwise would only put a procurement process between you and a Saturday morning.

05 — The small print, in plain words

What you are agreeing to.

Tax

Prices exclude sales tax

Every figure on this page is before any applicable sales or use tax. Tax is shown on the invoice or the Stripe receipt, not buried in the headline number.

Travel

Quoted separately

Rates cover work in and around New York. Travel beyond the metro area is quoted before you book, never added afterwards.

Retainer

It holds the date and comes off the total

A retainer is part payment, not a fee on top. The balance is due on delivery. Where a booking is invoiced instead, the whole fee is invoiced on net 30 terms.

Moving a date

Once, free, with notice

More than 14 days’ notice moves a booking once at no charge, subject to availability. Inside that window the retainer covers time reserved that cannot be filled at short notice. A child who is unwell is not a late cancellation — a family or newborn session moves at no charge, whenever you tell me.

Newborn

Booked to a due date, not a day

Newborn sessions hold a provisional week and confirm once the baby arrives. Pay the retainer against the due date and the week is yours.

Rights

Written down before the shoot

Personal sessions come with print rights for your own use. Commercial licensing is set out in the quotation. Either way it is in writing before the camera comes out — see rights and usage.
