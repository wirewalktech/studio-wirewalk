Studio / Work
Selected work

A gallery, not a showreel.

Personal work, made for its own sake: cityscape, landscape, water, people in public places, and the small things that reward going back to the same spot in different light. Commissioned work is largely absent for the ordinary reason — most of it is confidential, and the rest is shown only where the client has asked for it to be.

Want something in this register, for your own subject?

The look above comes from the same habits that produce good commercial work: turning up at the right hour, framing once, and doing the developing rather than leaving it to a preset. That transfers to a boardroom, a building or a product just as well as it does to a dune.
