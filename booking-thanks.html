Checking with the payment system…

One moment. This page asks Stripe what actually happened rather than assuming it worked, so it takes a second longer than a page that simply says “thank you”.

What happens now

  1. A receipt from Stripe, to the address you gave, within a minute or two. If it has not arrived, check the junk folder before assuming anything went wrong.
  2. Confirmation of the date from me, in writing, within one working day — with what is included, where we are meeting and what to expect on the day.
  3. The balance, where you paid a retainer or deposit, is due on delivery. Nothing is taken automatically; you will be invoiced.

Newborn sessions are held as a provisional week and confirmed once the baby arrives — the retainer holds the week, not a particular day.
