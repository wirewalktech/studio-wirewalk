What happens now

  1. A receipt from Stripe, to the address you gave, after payment clears. If it has not arrived, check the junk folder before assuming anything went wrong.
  2. Confirmation of the date from me, in writing, within one business day — with what is included, where we are meeting and what to expect on the day.
  3. The balance, where you paid a retainer or deposit, is due on delivery. Nothing is taken automatically; you will be invoiced.

Newborn sessions are held as a provisional week and confirmed once the baby arrives — the retainer holds the week, not a particular day. See the booking, cancellation and delivery terms.
